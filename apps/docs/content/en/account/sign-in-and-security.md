Manage your personal account and security settings from **Settings → My account**.

## Sign in and password

- Sign in with your organization email and password.
- Change your password anytime from **My account → Password**. Passwords are checked against minimum length and breach rules before saving.
- If you forget your password, request a reset link from the sign-in screen (when email is enabled) or ask an administrator for a one-time access link.

## Two-step verification (2FA)

- **Two-step verification**: select **Turn on**, enter your current password, scan the QR code with an authenticator app (TOTP), and confirm with a 6-digit code.
- **Recovery codes**: save your single-use recovery codes in a secure place outside your device.
- **Move to a new device**: while 2FA is active, start a new enrollment with your password and a current app or recovery code; your old device remains active until you confirm the new one.

:::note
Superadmins must always use 2FA. Organization administrators may also be required to use 2FA depending on the organization's security policy.
:::

## Active sessions

Under **My account → Sessions**, review all devices currently signed in to your account and revoke any session you no longer recognize.
