Cluster uses **Areas** and **Positions** to structure your organization, power the CONNECT org chart, and target mandatory GROW course assignments.

## Areas

Open **Settings → Areas**:

![Organization area tree with main nodes and member counts](/screenshots/admin-areas-en-light.png "Settings → Areas")

- **New main node**: create top-level areas (such as departments, divisions, or external units).
- **Sub-areas and reordering**: create child areas under any node, or drag and drop areas in the tree (with keyboard support and **Undo**) to reorganize or reorder siblings.
- **Area details**: select an area to view or change its **manager** (who receives scoped access to GROW compliance reports for their subtree), positions, and assigned people.

## Positions

Open **Settings → Positions**:

- Click **New position** to add a position to the catalog and optionally link it to an area.
- Archive or delete unused positions from the table actions.

## Assignments

Open **Settings → Assignments** (or drag people onto areas in the tree) to assign members to their area and position. When you assign a GROW course to an area or position, everyone in that group receives the assignment automatically.
