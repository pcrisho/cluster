Administrators, course managers, and area managers track progress from **Learning → Dashboard** and **Learning → Reports**.

## Dashboard

The **Dashboard** tab summarizes overall completion rates, overdue assignments, and pending quiz reviews at a glance.

## Compliance reports

In **Learning → Reports**:

![GROW compliance reports table with status, progress, due date, and CSV export](/screenshots/grow-reports-en-light.png "Learning → Reports")

- **Filter and sort**: filter by course, area, status (pending, in progress, overdue, completed), or due date; filters stay in the URL for easy sharing.
- **Person detail**: click any row to open the person's detail sheet, inspect their progress per step, and view or download their issued certificate.
- **CSV export**: export the filtered report as a localized CSV file.
- **Area-manager scope**: area managers see learners within the areas they manage, while organization administrators see the entire organization.
