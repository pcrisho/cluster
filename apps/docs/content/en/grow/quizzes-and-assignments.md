## Quizzes

Add a **Quiz** step to any section in the course outline:

- Define questions (**Single choice**, **Multiple choice** or **Written answer**), answer choices, the **Pass score**, and how many **Questions per attempt** are drawn.
- A **Written answer** is graded by hand: it waits in **Reviews** until an author grades it, and only then is the step marked complete.

![Course editor with a quiz step: questions, answer choices, pass score and questions per attempt](/screenshots/grow-quiz-builder-en-light.png "Learning → Course editor")

## Mandatory assignments

Click **Assign** on a published course to assign it to:

- Specific **people**,
- Entire **areas** (from the organization tree), or
- **Positions**.

![Assign dialog with people, areas, positions and a due date](/screenshots/grow-assign-en-light.png "Learning → Assign")

Set an optional **due date** to track overdue learners in compliance reports. When you assign to an area or position, members in that group receive the course in **My learning** and a pending count badge appears on **Courses** in the sidebar.
