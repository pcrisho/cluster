## My learning and Catalog

![GROW My learning view with a required course and recommendations from the catalog](/screenshots/grow-learning-en-light.png "Learning → My learning")

- **Learning → My learning**: lists your active, assigned, and completed courses, highlighting mandatory assignments and due dates.
- **Learning → Catalog**: browse published non-restricted courses available for self-enrollment.

## Course player

Open any enrolled course to launch the player:

- Navigate steps from the outline (on smaller screens, open the outline sheet).
- If **Sequential order** is enabled, locked steps unlock as you complete earlier ones.
- Complete text, video, file, and quiz steps to reach 100% completion.

## Certificates and public verification

When a course has **Issues certificate** enabled, completing all required steps generates a PDF certificate under **Learning → Certificates**.

- Each certificate includes the learner's name, course title, organization name at the time of issue, completion date, and your organization's accent color.
- Anyone can verify a certificate's authenticity using its public verification link (`/verify/<code>`), without signing in.
