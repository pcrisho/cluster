In **KNOW** (**Knowledge base**), pages are grouped into **Spaces**—for example, one space per team, department, or handbook.

![KNOW spaces overview with space cards and New space button](/screenshots/know-spaces-en-light.png "KNOW → Spaces")

## Creating a space

1. Open **KNOW** and click **New space** (available to administrators and members with the **Create spaces** permission).
2. Enter a **Name**, pick an icon, and choose **Who can open it** (open to everyone in the organization or restricted).
3. Once created, you can edit the space description collaboratively on the space home page.

## Space roles and sharing

Click **Share** inside any space to manage **General access** and per-principal permissions for specific people, areas, or positions:

| Role | What they can do |
|---|---|
| **Viewer** | Reads published pages and attached files. |
| **Editor** | Writes, edits, imports, and publishes pages. |
| **Manager** | Also edits space settings, files, and who has access. |

:::note
If you remove a person's access to a space while they are editing a page in real time, their live editing session is disconnected immediately.
:::
