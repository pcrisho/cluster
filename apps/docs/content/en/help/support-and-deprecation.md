## Supported releases

- **Current release**: receives bug fixes, security patches, and documentation updates.
- **Older releases**: once Cluster has active production deployments, security fixes will target the latest minor release. Self-hosted operators are encouraged to follow the [Updating Cluster](/en/self-host/update/) guide to stay current.

## Deprecation and removal

- During pre-1.0 development, unused or over-engineered features may be removed directly between releases.
- Once production deployments are active, any planned removal or breaking change will be announced in the **Changelog** at least **one release before** it is removed.
